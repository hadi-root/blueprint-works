Meet the Creator

Hi, I'm Hadi Muhammed, the developer and designer behind Blueprint Works.

What started as a hobby born out of curiosity quickly turned into a deep passion for building things on the internet. As a self-driven creator, I love the challenge of taking a blank screen and transforming it into a fully functional website or application.

For me, coding isn't just about syntax — it's about craftsmanship. I enjoy breaking down complex problems, designing intuitive user layouts, and writing clean, efficient code that brings ideas to life. Blueprint Works is the home for all my digital creations, experiments, and client projects.

When I'm not building websites or tinkering with new apps, you can usually find me exploring new design trends, reading about the latest in tech, and picking apart other people's sites to see how they're built.
