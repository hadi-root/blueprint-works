How a project runs

Four steps, in this order, so nothing is a surprise.

  1. 1

    Brief

    You tell us what it should do and who it is for.

  2. 2

    Blueprint

    We send a plan with layout, features and scope for your approval.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build to the plan and share a working version early.

  4. 4

    Launch

    We publish it, check it on real devices and hand it over.
